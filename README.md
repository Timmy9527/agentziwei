## Product overview (English)

Ziwei AI is an AI application based on the wisdom of traditional Chinese culture, specifically **Ziwei Dou Shu** (紫微斗数).

Unlike traditional fortune-telling or horoscope systems, Ziwei AI focuses on:

- long-term life patterns
- behavioral loops
- relationship dynamics
- decision pathways
- career and wealth structures

It helps users understand themselves, recognize recurring life patterns, and improve decisions through charting, automated analysis, and interactive reports.

### Core features

1. **Charting** — Birth charts, 10-year fortune, annual/monthly/daily/hourly charts. Combines historical, geographical, and astronomical context to visualize Ziwei star charts and trends.

2. **Chart analysis** — Automated, real-time analysis and reports. Systematic interpretation of chart results to support objective understanding and smarter life decisions.

3. **Interactive reports** — Conversational access to Ziwei knowledge; personalized, contextual interpretations for daily life.

4. **Life-pattern analysis** — Analyze long-term behavioral inertia, relationship structures, emotional cycles, and decision-making patterns.

5. **Cultural inheritance** — Fuses traditional wisdom with modern technology; promotes scientific readability and accessibility of Eastern metaphysics.

紫微AI是一款以中国传统文化智慧「紫微斗数」为基础的 AI 应用。

不同于传统算命或运势工具，紫微AI更关注：

- 长期人生模式
- 决策路径
- 行为循环
- 情绪与关系结构
- 财富与职业惯性

帮助用户通过结构化分析理解自己、识别长期问题模式，并优化人生决策。

### 核心功能

1. **排盘** — 出生一生排盘、大运十年、流年/流月/流日/流时排盘；结合历史、地理与天文学知识，掌握性格与天赋特征及各阶段趋势。

2. **解盘** — 全自动解盘与实时精准分析报告；程序化解读排盘结果，分析驱动力、路径结构与长期行为模式。

3. **对话报告** — 以互动对话方式学习传统文化，紫微斗数知识深入浅出、个性化与趣味化，贴近生活场景。

4. **人生分析** — 分析长期关系循环、职业惯性、财富模式与情绪结构，帮助用户理解重复出现的人生问题。

5. **文化传承与普及** — 传统文化与现代科技结合，以 AI 推理与结构分析提升命理知识的可读性与普及性。
